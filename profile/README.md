## What belongs here

Work that stands on its own, independent of any product: formal models,
empirical analysis, and the code that reproduces them. If a stranger could cite
it or build on it in a paper, it belongs here. If it exists to make a product
work, it belongs in [`sapians-hq`](https://github.com/sapians-hq).

The separation is not cosmetic. Research and product carry different licences,
different audiences, and different standards of proof — mixing them makes both
harder to trust.

## What we commit to

**Every repository here carries an explicit licence, and becomes public when the
work it supports is published.** A research namespace that publishes work nobody
may legally reuse claims an openness it does not deliver.

Work in progress stays private until the paper it belongs to is out — a preprint
competes with itself if the analysis circulates before it. Today that means
[`dlvt`](https://github.com/sapians-research/dlvt) is public under MIT; the other
two repositories are licensed and private, and open at publication.

**Every repository states how to cite it**, as a BibTeX block in its README.

**Every repository states its limits.** Where a model must not be used to make
decisions about individual people, the README says so — not only the paper.

**Claims carry their evidence.** Where evidence exists we cite it; where it does
not, we say so, and the language stays weak on purpose. That discipline, not
rhetorical force, is what makes the work defensible.

## Relationship to the product

[XRESET](https://xreset.sapians.com.br) is a structured occupational
self-assessment built by SAPIANS. Research published here may inform it, but the
direction is one-way and explicit: research artefacts are versioned and cited by
the product, never the reverse. No product data is used in this organisation.
